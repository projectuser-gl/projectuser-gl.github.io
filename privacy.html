Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

SayLang LLC ("SayLang", "we", "us") builds software products and integrations for third-party platforms. This policy explains what data we handle, why, and what choices you have.

Summary. This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics. Our applications and integrations process only the data needed to perform the function you asked for. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use customer data to train machine-learning models.

1. This website

The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set tracking cookies and we do not run analytics. Our host records standard server logs, including IP address and user agent, for security and reliability. If you email us, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you.

2. Applications and integrations

Where you install one of our applications, plugins or platform integrations, the data handled depends on the product:

  • Client-side products (for example browser or editor plugins) run entirely on your device. Files and content you open stay on your device and are not transmitted to us.
  • Platform integrations (for example CRM integrations) access only the objects and fields covered by the permissions you grant during installation, and only to perform the function described on the integration's listing.

We request the narrowest permission scopes a product needs. Where a platform offers OAuth, we use it rather than asking for account credentials, and we never ask you for your password.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use customer data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not combine data from one customer's account with another's.
  • We do not use customer data for advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Many of our products store nothing on our side. Where an integration must store data to work (for example synchronisation state or access tokens), we keep it only while the integration is installed, and delete it within 30 days of uninstallation or of a deletion request.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of infrastructure providers — hosting, source control, and the platform marketplaces our products are distributed through. These providers process data on our instructions only. We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Our products are intended for business and general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

9. Security

We limit access to production systems, use encrypted connections for data in transit, and keep the amount of data we hold as small as the product allows. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes affecting installed integrations will be announced on this page before they take effect.
